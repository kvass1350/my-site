**the long**
I started writing pieces for this blog the summer before uni, right around the same time I fell down a rabbit hole of personal blogs across the web. There's something fascinating about being able to learn about other people's lives and thoughts through their writing, despite never having met them in real life. The people behind those sites inspired me greatly -- so much so that I wanted to start a little site of my own, too (so I could shamelessly imitate them).

I now maintain this blog because I am trying to get better at [^1]writing, documenting things that I'm creating/building, and also so that whoever happens to wander onto this site (you!) can see what I am currently doing in case you ever want to reach out!

It’s great to have you here – I hope you enjoy taking a wander around. You might like to start by reading some of my [[1. writing|blog posts]], perusing my [[4. My art|art archive]], or just clicking on the links here and there to see where they lead you off to.
