Peering into the store, my eyes naturally drifted to the store owner. He glanced up briefly before returning his attention to his phone. Hidden behind cluttered shelves, he sat in the corner smoking while playing some video on his phone at medium volume. He wore a scrawny T-shirt and had an un-kept beard. 

We stepped inside. To the right was a wall of refrigerators stocked with a wide variety of drinks. We reached into the shelves and picked out a [^1]German beer that Google had recommended.

Back outside, I took a sip.The liquid ran down my throat. It was cold, wheaty, and sharply carbonated. After finishing the beer, we left the empty bottle in a crate by the street.
