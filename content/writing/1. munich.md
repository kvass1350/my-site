
Across the street, and beneath the sign, was a convenience store tucked between a row of small shops. As the little Ampelmann in the traffic lights flashed green, we crossed. Peering into the store, my eyes naturally drifted to the store owner. He glanced up briefly before returning his attention to his phone. Hidden behind cluttered shelves, he sat in the corner smoking while playing some video on his phone at medium volume. He wore a scrawny T-shirt and had an un-kept beard. 

We stepped inside. To the right was a wall of refrigerators stocked with a wide variety of drinks. We reached into the shelves and picked out a German beer that Google had recommended.

Back outside, I took a sip.The liquid ran down my throat. It was cold, wheaty, and sharply carbonated. After finishing the beer, we left the empty bottle in a crate by the street.

 It was my first time trying (German) beer!

It's been a few months since the trip. I must have lost much of my memory of it, including some of the most important parts. But here is my best recollection of one of the evenings.

It was raining in Munich, and my friends and I wanted to try some beer before leaving for Salzburg the next day. After sneaking out of the hotel and turning right onto the street, we noticed a buzzing LED sign across the road that read "Open 24 Hours".
 After that, we went back and played uno. 

 Obviously, beer was not allowed on the trip. But it was a lot of fun. It was my first time trying German beer (or drinking beer :o), and I liked it.
