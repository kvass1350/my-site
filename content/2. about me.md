**the long**
I started writing pieces for this blog the summer before uni. During that time, I somehow found myself stumbling across lots of personal blogs across the web. I find it fascinating to be able to learn about other people's lives and thoughts through their writing despite never having met them in real life. The creators of those sites inspired me greatly, which led me to want to start my own (so I could shamelessly imitate them). 

I now maintain this blog because I am trying to get better at [^1]writing, documenting things that I'm creating, and also so that whoever happens to wander onto this site (you!) can see what I am currently doing in case you ever want to reach out!

It’s great to have you here – I hope you enjoy taking a wander around. You might like to start by reading some of my [[1. writing|blog posts]], perusing my [[5. My art|art archive]], or just clicking on the links here and there to see where they take you, too. 

In case you’re wondering, this site is set up using Quartz + Obsidian, and I'm using GitHub to host.
