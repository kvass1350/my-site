### About me

**the short**
Hi, I'm Char. I take interest in making somewhat useless art. Apart from that, I've been attempting to learn how to build things that are supposed to be somewhat more useful. More often than not, they end up being even more useless :p

Welcome to my corner of the World Wide Web. I consider this a little backyard garden of mine on the internet, where I am still pruning, arranging, and planting stuff here and there.

My email is zcharlotte7890@gmail.com. Send me your favorite book, piece of music/art/story, or anything else. I would be elated you said hello!


**the long**
I started writing pieces for this blog the summer berfore uni. During that time, I found myself stumbling across lots of personal blogs. I found it fascinating to be able to learn about other people's lives and thoughts through their writing despite never having met them in real life. The creators of those sites inspired me greatly, which led me to want to start my own (so I could shamelessly imitate them). 

I now maintain this blog because I am trying to get better at [^1]writing, documenting things that I'm creating, and also so that the external viewer (you!) can see what I am currently doing in case you ever want to reach out!

It’s great to have you here – I hope you enjoy taking a wander around. You might like to start with reading some of my blog posts, perusing my art archive, or just clicking on the links here and there and see where they take you, too. 

In case you’re wondering, this site is set up using [^2]Quartz + Obsidian, and I'm using github to host.
