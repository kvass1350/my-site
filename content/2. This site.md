### this site 
what is this site? (this part is supposed to be reserved for the actual site...)

This site is a digital backyard garden where I dump things that I love, things I am making, and things I like to talk about. I hope that people who like similar things and want to talk about similar things will see them, and maybe I could come to know them if they happen to reach out. 

I try to keep this site as simple as possible, with minimal "design". My aim is to make the page as clean, readable, and easy to navigate as possible (I'd love suggestions on how I could improve this).

My art archive is slightly more "designed", I suppose, as I wanted to have a separate space where I could experiment with stuff and play with design choices more.
