### this site 
what is this site? (this part is supposed to be reserved for the actual site...)

This site is a digital backyard garden where I dump things that I love, things I am making, and things that I like to talk about. I hope that people who like similar things and want to talk about similar things will see them, and maybe I could come to know them if they happen to reach out. 

I try to keep this site as simple as possible, with minimal "design". My aim is to make the page as clean, readable and easy to navigate as possible (I'd love suggestions on how I could improve this!).

My art archive is slightly more "designed", I suppose, as I wanted to have a separate space where I get to experiment with stuff. 

To me, posting things online can sometimes feel embarrassing; putting my thoughts out into the wild, where strangers (not that I expect any visitors to this site, but anyway) can read them, sounds even more daunting and precarious. I am aware that some of the things I say can unintentionally sound utterly stupid—and worst of all, I'll have no way of knowing unless someone, or future me, informs me of it...Nevertheless, I still decided to publish this site. Perhaps one day I'll wake up and be struck by an epiphany of profound cringe, look back at all of this, and wonder what on earth I was thinking. Until that day comes, I'll keep maintaining it...

[^1]: I hope to gain more clarity through the process of compressing and organizing my thoughts into words and sentences with accuracy
