### this site 
what is this site? (at least what is the actual site is supposed to be...)

This site is a digital backyard garden where I dump things that I love, things I am making, and things that I like to talk about. I hope that people who like similar things and want to talk about similar things will see them, and maybe I could come to know them if they happen to reach out. 

I try to keep this site as simple as possible, with minimal "design". My aim is to make the page as clean, readable and easy to navigate as possible (I'd love suggestions on how I could improve this!).

My art archive is slightly more "designed", I suppose, as I wanted to have a separate space where I experiment with stuff. 

To me, posting things online can sometimes feel iffy(hm); putting my thoughts out into the wild where strangers (not that I expect any visitors on this site, but anyway) can read of them sounds even more daunting and precarious. I am aware that some of the things i say can unintentially sound utterly stupid -- and for the worst part, I'll have no way of knowing unless someone, or future me informs me so... I'm surely going to . nevertherless, after some mental battle, I still decided to create this site - I mean, if anything goes wrong, I'll just take everything down...Maybe this blog site will be a temporary thing, or maybe I will keep maintaining it.. :o

[^1]: I hope to gain more clarity through the process of compressing and orgainzing my thoughts into words and sentences with accuracy
